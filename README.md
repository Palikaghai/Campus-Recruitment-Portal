# Campus-Recruitment-Portal

## Problem Statement
* The campus recruitment process often involves multiple stakeholders such as students, recruiters, and placement officers, making it difficult to manage student profiles, resumes, placement drives, applications, interviews, and recruitment data efficiently.
* The Campus Recruitment Portal aims to provide a centralized platform that simplifies and organizes the complete campus recruitment process, allowing students to manage their profiles and applications, recruiters to manage recruitment opportunities, and placement officers to monitor and coordinate placement activities.

* Goal: To make the campus recruitment process more organized, accessible, and efficient through a single web-based platform

## Limitations of Existing System
The manual and spreadsheet-based approach described above carries a number of practical limitations that directly motivated the design of the Campus Recruitment Portal.
●	Lack of a centralised record: information about students, companies, drives, and applications is scattered across multiple files, increasing the risk of inconsistency and data loss.
●	No self-service visibility for students: students cannot check their own application or interview status and must depend on the placement office for updates.
●	Manual eligibility checking: placement staff must manually verify whether a student meets a drive's eligibility criteria (such as CGPA or branch), which is time-consuming and error-prone.
●	Inefficient resume handling: resumes submitted as email attachments are difficult to search, filter, or compare at scale.
●	Delayed communication: because updates are relayed manually, there is often a lag between a decision being made and the affected student being informed.
●	Limited reporting capability: generating statistics such as the number of students placed per branch or the average package offered requires manually consolidating data from multiple sources.
●	No audit trail: because much of the coordination happens over email or phone, there is no reliable, timestamped record of when a particular action was taken.
